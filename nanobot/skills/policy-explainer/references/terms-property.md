# Property Insurance Terms / 财产险术语

## Homeowner's Insurance / 家庭财产险 (家财险)

### What's typically covered

- Building structure (房屋主体结构)
- Interior decoration and fixtures (室内装修)
- Household contents and appliances (室内财产)
- Additional living expenses if home is uninhabitable (临时安置费用)

### What's typically excluded

- Cash, securities, precious metals, antiques, artwork (现金、有价证券、贵金属、古董、艺术品)
- Damage from normal wear and aging (正常磨损、老化)
- Land value (土地价值)
- Pets and livestock (宠物、牲畜)
- Illegal structures or modifications (违章建筑)

### Common covered perils

- Fire and explosion (火灾爆炸)
- Lightning, typhoon, flood, hail (雷击、台风、洪水、冰雹)
- Burst pipes and water damage (水管爆裂)
- Theft with visible forced entry (盗窃，需有明显破入痕迹)
- Aircraft/vehicle impact (飞行物坠落、外来车辆撞击)

> 家财险的保额按实际价值定，不是"想保多少保多少"。100 万的房子保 200 万，理赔时还是按实际损失赔。

## Commercial Property Insurance / 企业财产险

### Insured property categories

| Category | Examples | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Fixed assets | Buildings, machinery, equipment | Insured at replacement or book value |
| Inventory | Raw materials, finished goods | Insured at actual value |
| Other | Office furniture, tools | Specify in schedule |

### Business Interruption / 营业中断险

- Covers lost revenue during the period business is shut down due to an insured peril
- Requires underlying property policy
- Indemnity period: time to restore operations (typically 6-12 months)

> 营业中断险赔的不是修房子的钱（那是财产险赔的），而是修房子期间你没法营业损失的收入。

## Liability Extensions / 责任险附加

### Public Liability / 公众责任险

- Covers bodily injury or property damage to third parties occurring at the insured premises
- Common for shops, restaurants, offices, event venues

### Employer's Liability / 雇主责任险

- Covers employee injuries at work that employer is legally liable for
- Complements workers' compensation (工伤保险)

## Common Customer Questions

### "Pipe burst and damaged my downstairs neighbor's ceiling — does my policy cover that?"

Two parts:
1. Your own damage: covered under water damage peril in your homeowner's policy
2. Neighbor's damage: only if your policy includes third-party liability coverage (第三方责任). Many basic policies do NOT include this — check the rider list.

### "Is earthquake covered?"

Standard homeowner's policies in China typically EXCLUDE earthquake (地震). Some insurers offer earthquake as an optional rider at additional premium. Government catastrophe insurance schemes exist in pilot areas.

### "My rental property was damaged — tenant or landlord's policy?"

Depends on what was damaged:
- Building structure → landlord's policy
- Tenant's personal belongings → tenant's policy (if they have one)
- Damage caused by tenant's negligence → tenant may be liable; landlord's policy may cover with subrogation rights against tenant
