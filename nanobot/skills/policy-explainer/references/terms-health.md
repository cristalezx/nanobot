# Health Insurance Terms / 健康险术语

## Insurance Types Overview

| Type | Chinese | Typical Term | Key Feature |
|------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Medical insurance | 医疗险 | 1 year | Reimburses actual medical expenses |
| Critical illness | 重疾险 | 20-30yr/lifetime | Lump-sum payout on diagnosis |
| Cancer-specific | 防癌险 | Varies | Only covers cancer, lower premium |
| Accident medical | 意外医疗 | 1 year | Injury-related medical costs only |

## Medical Insurance / 医疗险

### Million-dollar Medical / 百万医疗险

- Covers hospitalization expenses above deductible (usually ¥10,000/year)
- Annual coverage limit typically ¥1M-¥6M
- Covers: hospitalization, special outpatient (chemo, dialysis, etc.), day surgery
- Typically does NOT cover: outpatient visits, dental, maternity (unless rider)

> 百万医疗的 1 万免赔额意思是：住院花了 3 万，社保报了 1.5 万，剩下 1.5 万再减去 1 万免赔额，保险赔 5000 元。

### Key terms

- **Social insurance first (有社保/无社保)**: Premium differs; without social insurance, reimbursement ratio drops (e.g., 60% instead of 100%)
- **Guaranteed renewal (保证续保)**: Policy cannot be cancelled due to claims or health changes during guaranteed period (common: 6/10/15/20 years)
- **Hospital grade requirement (医院要求)**: Usually requires 二级及以上公立医院 (public hospital, grade 2+)

## Critical Illness Insurance / 重疾险

### How it works

- Policyholder gets diagnosed with a covered critical illness
- Insurer pays the full sum insured as a lump sum — no bills needed
- Money can be used freely (medical, living expenses, income replacement)

> 重疾险赔的是"人"，不是"病"。确诊就给钱，你拿去治病、养家、还房贷都行。

### Coverage tiers

| Tier | Chinese | Typical conditions |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| Critical illness | 重症 | Cancer, heart attack, stroke, etc. (25 standard + extras) |
| Moderate illness | 中症 | Less severe versions of critical conditions |
| Mild illness | 轻症 | Early-stage conditions (e.g., carcinoma in situ) |

### Common exclusions

- Pre-existing conditions not disclosed during underwriting (未如实告知的既往症)
- Conditions diagnosed during waiting period
- Self-inflicted injury
- AIDS (unless from blood transfusion or occupational exposure)

## Pre-existing Conditions / 既往症

Definition varies by insurer, but generally includes:
1. Conditions diagnosed before policy effective date
2. Conditions with symptoms that a reasonable person would have sought treatment for
3. Conditions being treated or monitored at time of application

> "既往症"不只是已确诊的病。如果投保前体检发现异常但没去看，也可能被认定为既往症。

## Health Disclosure / 健康告知

- Must answer truthfully — failure may void the policy
- Only need to answer what's asked (询问告知制) — no need to volunteer extra info
- If unsure, disclose rather than omit

## Common Customer Questions

### "My claim was denied because of a pre-existing condition I didn't know about"

If the condition was genuinely unknown and not asked about in the health questionnaire, the customer may have grounds to appeal. Suggest formal appeal or escalate to claims review team.

### "What's the difference between 百万医疗 and 重疾险?"

百万医疗 reimburses hospital bills (expense-based). 重疾险 pays a lump sum on diagnosis (income-replacement). They complement each other — 百万医疗 covers the hospital bill, 重疾险 covers everything else (lost income, recovery, etc.).
