# Auto Insurance Terms / 车险术语

## Mandatory: Traffic Compulsory Insurance / 交强险

- Required by law for all vehicles
- Covers third-party bodily injury and property damage ONLY
- Fixed premium, fixed limits (death/disability: ¥180,000, medical: ¥18,000, property: ¥2,000)
- Does NOT cover the policyholder's own vehicle or injuries

> 交强险只管你撞了别人，不管你自己的车。额度也不高，所以大家都会加商业险。

## Commercial Auto Insurance / 商业车险

### Vehicle Damage Insurance / 车损险 (merged since 2020 reform)

Now includes what used to be separate coverages:
- Vehicle damage (车辆损失)
- Theft (全车盗抢)
- Glass breakage (玻璃单独破碎)
- Spontaneous combustion (自燃)
- Engine flooding (涉水)
- Uninsured motorist (无法找到第三方)
- Scratches — only if bundled (划痕险，部分公司)

Common exclusions:
- Normal wear and tear (自然磨损)
- Tire-only damage without other vehicle damage (单独轮胎损坏)
- Driving under the influence (酒驾/毒驾)
- Vehicle used for purposes not declared (改变使用性质，如私车跑营运)

### Third-Party Liability / 第三方责任险 (三者险)

- Covers damage/injury caused to OTHERS beyond compulsory limits
- Recommended coverage: ¥1M-¥3M in urban areas
- Does NOT cover policyholder, insured vehicle, or passengers in insured vehicle

> 三者险是交强险的"加强版"，建议至少买 100 万。城市里撞个豪车或撞伤人，交强险那点额度根本不够。

### Driver & Passenger Seat Insurance / 车上人员责任险 (座位险)

- Covers injuries to driver and/or passengers in the insured vehicle
- Purchased per seat, common amounts: ¥10,000-¥50,000 per seat

## Common Customer Questions

### "No-claim discount" / 无赔优待 (NCD)

- No claims in year 1: ~30% discount next year
- Consecutive no-claim years accumulate more discount
- One claim resets or reduces the discount

### "Can I claim for parking lot scratches with no witness?"

Yes, under the merged vehicle damage insurance with 无法找到第三方 coverage. File a police report first.

### "I lent my car to a friend who crashed it — am I covered?"

Generally yes, if the friend has a valid license and was not under the influence. The policy follows the vehicle, not the driver. But if the friend is explicitly excluded in the policy, then no.
