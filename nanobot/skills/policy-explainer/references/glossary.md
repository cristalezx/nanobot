# Insurance Glossary / 保险术语对照表

Quick-reference for the most commonly asked-about terms. Each entry: Chinese term, English equivalent, plain-language explanation, and a concrete example.

## Premium / 保费

Customer pays this to keep the policy active. Think of it as a "membership fee" for protection.

> 每月交 300 元保费，就像每月交话费一样，保障才持续有效。

## Deductible / 免赔额

The amount the customer pays out of pocket BEFORE insurance kicks in.

- **Absolute deductible (绝对免赔额)**: Fixed amount, e.g. first ¥500 is on you
- **Relative deductible (相对免赔额)**: If loss exceeds threshold, insurer pays ALL of it

> 免赔额 500 元 = 损失 2000 元时，保险公司赔 1500 元。

## Sum Insured / 保额

Maximum amount the insurer will pay. NOT necessarily the amount you'll receive — actual payout depends on loss.

> 保额 50 万不代表一定赔 50 万，而是最多赔 50 万。

## Waiting Period / 等待期

Period after policy starts during which claims are not accepted. Common in health and critical illness insurance.

- Health insurance: typically 30-90 days
- Critical illness: typically 90-180 days

> 等待期就像新手机的"不退换期"——刚买的保险还不能马上用。

## Exclusions / 责任免除 (免责条款)

Specific situations the policy does NOT cover. Always highlighted in the policy document.

Common universal exclusions:
- Intentional acts (故意行为)
- Illegal activities (违法犯罪)
- War and nuclear events (战争、核事故)
- Pre-existing conditions in health insurance (既往症)

> 这些情况保险不管，就像质保不覆盖人为损坏一样。

## Benefit Period / 保障期间

How long the policy provides coverage. Not the same as payment period.

- 1-year term (短期险): renew annually
- Long-term (长期险): 20/30 years or lifetime

## Grace Period / 宽限期

Extra time (usually 60 days) to pay overdue premium before policy lapses. Coverage continues during grace period.

> 忘交保费不会马上失效，有 60 天"缓冲期"。

## Cash Value / 现金价值

The "savings" portion in a life insurance policy. Relevant for surrender (退保) calculations.

> 退保拿回的不是已交保费，而是现金价值，通常前几年远低于已交金额。

## Rider / 附加险

Optional add-on coverage attached to a main policy. Cannot be purchased standalone.

> 附加险就像点外卖时加的小食，必须有主菜才能加。

## Subrogation / 代位求偿

After paying a claim, the insurer takes over the customer's right to pursue the responsible third party.

> 保险公司先赔你，然后替你去找肇事方要钱。

## Claim / 理赔

The process of requesting payment under the policy after a covered event occurs.

## Underwriting / 核保

Insurer's process of evaluating risk before issuing or renewing a policy. May involve health questionnaires, inspections, etc.

## Endorsement / 批单

A formal amendment to an existing policy (e.g., change of address, add a driver, adjust coverage).
